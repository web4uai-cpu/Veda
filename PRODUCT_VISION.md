Version: 1.0

# Vision Statement

VEDA is a Knowledge Operating System for Sanatan Dharma.

Its purpose is to make the wisdom of the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Puranas, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Vedanta traditions, and Sanskrit commentaries accessible, searchable, explainable, and interconnected through modern artificial intelligence and knowledge graph technologies.

VEDA is not a chatbot.

VEDA is not merely a search engine.

VEDA is a living knowledge network that helps users discover, understand, compare, and research the spiritual, philosophical, historical, and cultural foundations of Sanatan Dharma.

---

# Mission

To build the world's most comprehensive and trustworthy digital knowledge platform for Sanatan Dharma.

The platform should enable anyone—from beginners to scholars—to explore authentic sources, understand concepts in context, discover relationships between scriptures, and conduct deep research without losing connection to original references.

# Core Problem

Knowledge related to Sanatan Dharma is fragmented across:

* Thousands of books
* Multiple translations
* Different schools of philosophy
* Diverse interpretations
* Isolated websites
* Offline archives
* Academic papers
* Traditional commentaries

Users often face:

* Difficulty finding authentic references
* Lack of context
* Contradictory explanations
* Missing citations
* Limited cross-linking between concepts
* Poor search experiences

Current AI systems frequently generate answers without verifiable references.

This reduces trust and academic usefulness.

---

# Product Thesis

The future of spiritual knowledge systems is not conversational AI.

The future is:

Knowledge Graph + Retrieval + Reasoning + Citation

Instead of generating isolated answers, VEDA should connect ideas across scriptures and reveal how concepts evolve through different texts, traditions, and commentaries.

---

# Product Principles

## Principle 1

Scripture First

Primary scriptures always take priority over AI-generated explanations.

Order of trust:

1. Original Scripture
2. Traditional Commentary
3. Verified Scholarly Source
4. AI Explanation

---

## Principle 2

Evidence Required

Every answer must be supported by references whenever possible.

No unsupported scriptural claims.

---

## Principle 3

Multiple Perspectives

When traditions differ, VEDA should present all major viewpoints rather than selecting a single interpretation.

Examples:

* Advaita
* Vishishtadvaita
* Dvaita
* Kashmir Shaivism
* Yoga Philosophy
* Sankhya

---

## Principle 4

Transparency

The system must distinguish between:

* Scripture
* Commentary
* Interpretation
* User Uploaded Content
* AI Reasoning

---

## Principle 5

Knowledge Discovery

Users should discover concepts through exploration, not only through direct questions.
